Privacy Policy

Burton Algorithms, Inc. Effective 20 October 2026.

This policy explains what personal data Burton Algorithms, Inc. ("Burton", "we") collects, why we use it, and the choices you have. It covers burtonalgorithms.com and the internal tools our team uses to publish and manage social media content, including tools that connect to LinkedIn.

Who we are

Burton Algorithms, Inc. is a Delaware corporation. Our registered office is 651 N Broad St, Suite 206, Middletown, DE 19709, United States. You can reach us at business@burtonalgorithms.com.

Data we collect

  • Contact details you send us. Your name, email address and message when you use our contact form or email us.
  • LinkedIn data, only for members who connect their own account. When a team member signs in with LinkedIn and grants permission, we receive their basic profile (name, profile photo, email), the posts they publish through our tool, comments and reactions on those posts, and post statistics such as impressions. We receive only what the member approves on LinkedIn's consent screen.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, kept for security and to keep the service running.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries.
  • To publish and schedule posts for the connected member, show them the comments on their own posts, let them reply, and show them how their posts perform.
  • To keep our systems secure and working.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use LinkedIn data for advertising, for building marketing lists, or for any purpose the connected member has not asked for.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers that host and run our systems, under agreements that require them to protect it, or when the law requires it. LinkedIn data is shown only to the member it belongs to and to the Burton team members who manage that member's content with their permission.

How long we keep it

We keep contact messages for as long as we need them to answer you and for our business records. We keep LinkedIn data only while the member's account stays connected. When a member disconnects, or asks us to delete their data, we delete the LinkedIn data we stored for them within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to stop using it, by emailing business@burtonalgorithms.com. You can also remove our access at any time in your LinkedIn settings under permitted services. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law, such as the GDPR or the CCPA, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

Security

We protect data with access controls, encrypted connections and limited staff access. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
